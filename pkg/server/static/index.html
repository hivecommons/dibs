    Why not just open an issue?

    Most issues filed into open-source queues are ignored, misunderstood, or never implemented at all. DIBS exists to raise the odds your idea gets used without you building it.

    MARKET REACHDiscovery

    Your idea is scored against every opted-in repo. No guessing where to file.

    EXECUTION GUARANTEEAccepted = implemented

    A repo that accepts your idea has agent capacity ready to build it.

    DIVIDENDSCredit, in writing

    You file the issue under your own name. Attribution is native and permanent.

    How it works
    1

    Write the idea

    A title and a short body: the problem, who benefits, what done looks like. The idea is scored against every opted-in repo.

    2

    Match and offer

    Review matched repos and offer the idea to the ones you choose. Both sides must accept.

    3

    File the issue

    On acceptance, DIBS builds a prefilled GitHub issue. You file it from your own account with the ideated label.

    The all-contributors spec credits ideas. The CRediT taxonomy credits conceptualization. DIBS records that credit publicly.

    For repos

    Opt a repo in. Set topics and appetite. Review incoming ideas.

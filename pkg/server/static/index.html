    How it works

    Like dating, minus the heartbreak: your idea matches a repo, they accept, you get the credit — in writing.

    1

    Write the idea

    A title and a short write-up: the problem, who benefits, what “done” looks like. An LLM distills a TLDR and scores its fit against every opted-in repo.

    2

    Match & offer

    Swipe through matched repos and offer the idea to the ones you choose. Both sides must say yes.

    3

    File it — with your name on it

    On acceptance, Dibs hands you a prefilled GitHub issue. You file it from your own account, labeled ideated. Natively attributed to you, forever.

    Open source has always credited ideas — the all-contributors spec has a 💡 “ideas” emoji; academia's CRediT taxonomy credits “Conceptualization”. Dibs makes that credit real and public: creators get credit, agents do the work, projects take the bow.
